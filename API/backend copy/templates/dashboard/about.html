Our Mission

At KINÉRA FILM FIXERS, we bridge the gap between international film productions and authentic African stories. Based in Cotonou, Benin, our mission is to offer seamless production support across the continent.

Our fixers have a thorough mastery of diverse production environments — whether urban, rural, remote, or sensitive locations — and are fully capable of handling all logistics, permits, and unforeseen challenges.

"We transform ambitious projects into seamless and successful experiences while preserving the authenticity and richness of African stories."
